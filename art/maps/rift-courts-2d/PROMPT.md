## 實際提示詞

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Create the actual clean, game-ready 2D background illustration for “Rift Courts”, an original portrait medieval fantasy PvP arena, in a 4:5 vertical master canvas. Output a single edge-to-edge illustrated map.

Image 1 is the exact spatial paintover guide. Keep the same canvas, crop, scale, and top-down 2D screen-space silhouette. Paint right on its silhouettes and color regions, with no repositioning. Image 2 is the style reference only, the prince turnaround. Match the painterly finish of that character: weighty rounded shapes, controlled warm ochre gold edge highlights, deep navy cloth, warm brown shadows, softly painted blue-grey recesses, carved details, substantial material feel with hand-painted gouache brush marks, readable edges. Translate those visual qualities to buildings, flags, and ground. No characters in the output.

Layout: Two identical-size, wide, empty rectangular stone courts directly above and below each other, both of equal visual height, front-to-back size, and width; vertical sides stay parallel. Terracotta / faded burgundy in the upper court, deep royal blue in the lower. Paint the exact three vertical bridges, in the center of the image, with wide dark water gaps clearly visible between them. Follow Image 1 closely: west and east stone bridges about the same length, inset inside the side edges of the courts, and a wider, shorter center bridge with two rectangular approaches and a low-profile dark bronze iron trap grille in the middle. Paint the small gold-edged faded heraldic roundels flat into the ground at the marked positions. Upper and lower entrance walls are thin and near the picture edges. Keep cliff edges and small pine accents outside the play surfaces; the center 54% of the canvas width contains all the gameplay. Both courts have the same paving and empty deployment space. No objects across a bridge or on a court. All gameplay surfaces align to the flat horizontal image plane, no perspective convergence, no camera rotation. Keep all ramps and bridge interfaces flush and obvious.

Art direction: polished 2D hand-painted fantasy strategy game background, visibly painted brush strokes and nuanced stacked colors. Warm grey aging limestone, softly hand-painted stone slabs with restrained texture and quiet seams, dark blue-green shaded ravine with softly painted rocky ledges, sporadic muted olive pine clusters. Distinct narrow painted rail edges, chipped masonry, and colored pennants and restrained antique-gold accents focus the palette. Small-scale heraldic floor decor; the arena stays quiet enough for the existing characters to read at phone size. Flat floor articulation. Keep exterior edges organically brushed, with rough umber rocks and plants softly falling into dark teal shadow. Keep the same depth and scale across the canvas. The full height of the guide must remain visible. Need a beautiful finished game environment illustration, crisp at mobile scale with daylight modeled by hand in paint. No look of a 3D render or 3D model, no perspective vanishing point, no extrusion, no isometric diagonals, no hard polygon facets, no black outlines, no vectors, no pixel art, no huge towers, no border, no UI, no text, no watermark.
```
